# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct.html).

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment include:

- Demonstrating empathy and kindness toward other people.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback.
- Accepting responsibility and apologizing for mistakes.
- Focusing on what is best for the community.

Examples of unacceptable behavior include:

- Sexualized language or imagery and unwanted sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing another person's private information without permission.
- Other conduct reasonably considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing acceptable behavior.

They may remove, edit, or reject comments, issues, pull requests, code contributions, or other material that violates this Code of Conduct.

Moderation decisions should be proportionate to the circumstances and communicated when appropriate.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within QuotaWeave community spaces and when individuals officially represent the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

Instances of unacceptable behavior may be reported privately to the project maintainer through the contact information on the [maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/djredflame).

Please do not include private personal information in public issues.

Reports will be considered fairly, with attention to the privacy and safety of those involved. Review times depend on maintainer availability.

Security vulnerabilities should instead follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Enforcement Guidelines

Depending on severity, context, and previous behavior, responses may include:

### Correction

A private warning or request to correct inappropriate behavior.

### Warning

A formal warning explaining the violation and expectations for future participation.

### Temporary Ban

A temporary restriction from participation in project community spaces.

### Permanent Ban

A permanent restriction for serious or repeated violations.

The maintainer will consider the impact of the behavior, the safety of participants, and proportionality when deciding on enforcement.
